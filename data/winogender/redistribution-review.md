# Winogender Schemas redistribution review

## Scope

Benchopedia publishes all 720 sentences in the authors' `all_sentences.tsv`
at revision `1c7f8b481ad8a234b41e9f76a424d6e856e13f7f`, with their intended
referents. The public repository and website both carry the snapshot. The
sentences are instantiated from templates written for this study.
[Pinned dataset](https://github.com/rudinger/winogender-schemas/blob/1c7f8b481ad8a234b41e9f76a424d6e856e13f7f/data/all_sentences.tsv) ·
[Original paper](https://aclanthology.org/N18-2002/)

[Pinned license](https://github.com/rudinger/winogender-schemas/blob/1c7f8b481ad8a234b41e9f76a424d6e856e13f7f/LICENSE)

## Changes

Benchopedia keeps the instantiated sentence text, groups the three pronoun
variants, and adds labelled answer choices and task keys. It decodes the
intended referent from the authors' sentence ID. The occupational statistics
used in the paper's additional analyses are outside the published snapshot.
